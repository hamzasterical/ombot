# App or login issue

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

| Policy ID | Owner | Risk tier | Version | Review cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| policy_app_login | Digital Banking Support | Low | 1.6 | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers technical problems with the OmBank mobile app and online banking: crashes, slowness, error messages, forgotten passwords and failed logins. If the customer reports changes they did not make, or logins they don't recognise, use the account takeover policy instead.

**How customers describe it:** "the app keeps crashing"; "I forgot my password"; "I can't log in".

## 2. Common causes
- An outdated app version or operating system
- An expired session, or biometrics reset after a phone update
- Network problems (weak signal, VPN, public Wi-Fi blocking the app)
- Device incompatibility, or a rooted or jailbroken device, which the app blocks for security
- The account locked after 5 failed login attempts
- A known service incident or planned maintenance

## 3. Mandatory checks
1. Confirm the app version, device model and operating system.
2. Check the service status page for known incidents or planned maintenance.
3. Check whether the account is locked for security reasons, and why. A security lock is not lifted through this policy.
4. Check security events for signs of takeover (new device, password change the customer did not make). If found, reclassify.

## 4. Standard response
Share basic troubleshooting:
- Update the app to the latest version and restart the device.
- Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and turn off any VPN.
- Reset the password through the official "Forgot password" flow in the app.
- Re-enable biometrics in the app settings after logging in with the password.
- If the account is locked after failed attempts, it unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or immediately through the reset flow.

## 5. Customer communication
- Never ask for the PIN, password or OTP, including "to test" the login.
- Never send login links by SMS or email in reply to a complaint; direct the customer to the official app or website.
- If there is a known incident, apologise, say it is being fixed, and give an estimated recovery time if one is published.

## 6. Timelines (sample)
- Automatic unlock after failed attempts: 30 minutes.
- Known incidents are posted on the status page within 30 minutes of detection.
- Unresolved technical cases are passed to Digital Banking Support within 1 business day.

## 7. Escalation triggers
- Signs of account takeover: reclassify as critical.
- The customer could not make an urgent payment because of an outage: escalate, and consider refunding any late fees.
- The same error is reported by many customers: report it as a possible incident.

## 8. Record keeping
Record the device details, troubleshooting given and outcome for 2 years.
