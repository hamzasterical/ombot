# Fees and charges question

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

| Policy ID | Owner | Risk tier | Version | Review cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| policy_fees_charges | Retail Products | Low | 4.1 | Every 6 months, and on every fee change |

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers customer questions and complaints about fees, charges and limits on OmBank retail and premier checking and savings accounts. It sets out the published fee schedule, when fees can be waived or refunded, and who may approve a refund.

**How customers describe it:** "what is the transfer fee"; "why was I charged a service fee"; "what is the fee for an instant transfer or a wire".

## 2. Common causes
- The customer is unaware of the fee schedule or of a recent change to it
- Maintenance, transfer, overdraft or ATM fees
- The customer no longer meets the conditions for a fee waiver (for example, the balance dropped below the minimum)
- A fee applied in error (system error, wrong account tier, or a fee charged twice)

## 3. Mandatory checks
1. Identify the fee type, the amount and the date it was charged.
2. Compare it with the published fee schedule below, for the customer's account tier.
3. Check whether a waiver condition applied (average balance, direct deposit, Premier tier).
4. Check whether the same fee was refunded as a courtesy in the last 12 months.

## 4. Standard response
Explain the fee and link to the fee schedule. Tell the customer how to avoid it in future, for example how to qualify for the maintenance fee waiver or where to find in-network ATMs. If the fee was applied in error, forward to a human for correction.

## 6. Limits (sample)
- Instant transfers: up to $5,000 per transaction and $10,000 per day.
- Cash withdrawals: up to $1,000 per day.
- Online card payments: up to $3,000 per transaction.

## 7. Refund and waiver rules
| Situation | Action | Who may approve |
|---|---|---|
| Fee charged in error | Full refund | Customer service officer (human approval required) |
| First overdraft fee in 12 months | One courtesy refund of up to $35 | Customer service officer |
| Repeated fees, customer in financial hardship | Refund up to $105 and refer to the hardship team | Customer service team lead |
| Premier annual fee, account downgraded within 30 days of the charge | Pro-rata refund | Customer service team lead |
| Fee correctly charged, no special circumstances | Explain; no refund | Automated reply allowed |

## 8. Customer communication
- Quote the exact fee, the date and the schedule line it comes from.
- Be neutral: do not suggest the customer was at fault.
- Do not promise a refund in an automated reply; say a human will review a fee charged in error.

## 9. Escalation triggers
- Signs of financial hardship (repeated overdrafts, missed payments, the customer mentions difficulty): refer to the hardship team.
- The fee does not match the schedule: report it as a possible system error.

## 10. Record keeping
Keep fee refund decisions for 7 years.
