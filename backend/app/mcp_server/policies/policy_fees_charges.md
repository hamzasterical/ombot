# Fees and charges question

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

## Common causes
- Customer unaware of the fee schedule
- Maintenance, transfer or ATM fees

## Standard checks
- Identify the fee type and the date it was charged
- Compare it with the published fee schedule

## Standard response
Explain the fee and link to the fee schedule. If the fee was applied in error, forward to a human for correction.

## Fee schedule (sample, USD)
| Fee | Retail checking | Premier checking |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly maintenance fee | $12.00, waived with an average balance of $1,500+ or a monthly direct deposit of $500+ | $0 |
| Premier package annual service fee | n/a | $150.00, charged once a year in the account anniversary month |
| Out-of-network ATM fee (per withdrawal, any ATM not owned by the bank) | $2.50 | $2.50 (in-network ATMs are free on both tiers) |
| Overdraft fee (per item, max 3 per day) | $35.00 | $35.00 |
| Domestic standard transfer | Free | Free |
| Instant transfer | 1% of the amount, min $0.50, max $10.00 | Free |
| International wire (outgoing) | $25.00 plus any intermediary bank charges | $15.00 plus any intermediary bank charges |
| Card replacement | $5.00 (free when reported lost or stolen) | Free |
| Closing a savings or checking account | Free after 90 days; $25.00 if closed within 90 days of opening | Same |

## Limits (sample)
- Instant transfers: up to $5,000 per transaction and $10,000 per day.
- Cash withdrawals: up to $1,000 per day.
- Online card payments: up to $3,000 per transaction.
