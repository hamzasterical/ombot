# Account takeover

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

## Common causes
- Phishing or OTP disclosure
- SIM swap
- Malware on the customer's device
- Reused or leaked password

## Standard checks
- Review recent logins, devices and profile changes
- Check for newly added beneficiaries
- Lock the profile and force a credential reset
- Review outgoing transactions since the first suspicious login

## Standard response
A human officer secures the account, resets credentials through a verified channel, and walks the customer through next steps.
