# General frustration or feedback

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

| Policy ID | Owner | Risk tier | Version | Review cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| policy_general_feedback | Customer Experience | Low | 1.3 | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers general feedback, praise, feature requests and expressions of frustration that do not report a financial loss or a security issue. Every message is still treated as a complaint until checked, because customers sometimes mention a serious problem in passing.

**How customers describe it:** "I am annoyed by the wait times"; "please add a new feature"; "the new app design is confusing".

## 2. Common causes
- Long waiting times in branches or on the phone
- Interface changes in the app
- Unmet feature expectations
- Staff behaviour or service quality
- Branch closures or changed opening hours

## 3. Mandatory checks
1. Check that the message contains no hidden report of lost money, fraud, a security problem or a vulnerable situation (bereavement, illness, financial hardship, coercion). If it does, reclassify.
2. Record the feedback theme (wait times, app, staff, product, branch) for monthly reporting.
3. Note whether the customer asks for a reply or a callback.

## 4. Standard response
Thank the customer, acknowledge the frustration, and say the feedback has been recorded for the product team. Where possible, add something useful, such as how to book a branch appointment or where to find a feature in the app.

## 5. Customer communication
- Be warm and specific to what the customer said; avoid generic template wording.
- Do not promise that a feature will be built or a policy changed.
- Do not argue with the customer's view.
- If the customer is unhappy with how the complaint was handled, tell them they can ask for a review by a senior officer.

## 6. Timelines (sample)
- Acknowledgement within 1 business day.
- Feedback themes are reported to product owners monthly.

## 7. Escalation triggers
- A hidden financial or security issue: reclassify.
- A complaint about a named staff member's conduct: refer to the branch or team manager.
- The customer threatens to leave the bank or to complain to a regulator: pass to a senior officer.
- Signs of vulnerability: refer to the customer care team.

## 8. Record keeping
Keep feedback records for 2 years for trend analysis.
