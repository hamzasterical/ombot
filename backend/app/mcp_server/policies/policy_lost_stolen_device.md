# Lost or stolen device or card

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

| Policy ID | Owner | Risk tier | Version | Review cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| policy_lost_stolen_device | Card Services | Critical | 2.1 | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers a customer reporting that a debit card, or a phone or other device with the OmBank app or a stored card, has been lost or stolen. The aim is to stop further losses first, then restore access. Any transaction made after the loss is handled under the unauthorized transaction policy.

**How customers describe it:** "my phone was stolen"; "I lost my debit card"; "I left my wallet in a taxi".

## 2. Definitions
- **Block:** a permanent stop on a card. A blocked card cannot be unblocked; a new card is issued.
- **Temporary lock:** a reversible pause the customer can apply in the app while they look for a misplaced card.
- **Session revocation:** logging out the app on a device and removing it from the trusted devices list, which also disables the digital wallet card on that device.

## 3. Common causes
- Theft, including pickpocketing and phone snatching
- Loss on public transport or in taxis
- Misplaced at home or at work (a temporary lock is often enough)

## 4. Mandatory steps
1. Record the time of the report and the customer's estimate of when the loss happened.
2. Block the card and revoke app sessions on the lost device (human approval required).
3. Check for transactions after the reported loss time, including contactless and digital wallet payments.
4. Verify the customer's identity through a secondary channel before changing any contact details or registering a new device.
5. Confirm the delivery address for the replacement card. If the address changed in the last 30 days, verify it first.

## 5. Authority matrix
| Action | Who may decide |
|---|---|
| Read-only checks, draft reply | Automated agent |
| Block card, revoke sessions | Card services officer (human approval required) |
| Order a replacement card to the address on file | Card services officer |
| Send a replacement to a new address | Card services officer, after secondary verification |

## 6. Customer communication
- A human officer blocks the card and sessions, confirms what was protected, and arranges a replacement card or device registration.
- Tell the customer exactly what was blocked and what still works (for example, other cards on the account).
- If transactions happened after the loss, explain that they will be investigated separately, and do not promise a refund at this stage.
- Never ask for the PIN, password or OTP. Advise the customer to report a stolen phone to their mobile carrier and the police.

## 7. Timelines (sample)
- Blocking a card or revoking app sessions takes effect immediately.
- A replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days, free of charge when reported lost or stolen.
- Express delivery in 2 business days is available on request (free for Premier customers).

## 8. Escalation triggers
- Transactions after the loss time: open an unauthorized transaction case.
- The customer reports the PIN was written down or kept with the card: note this, but it does not stop the investigation.
- A customer with no other means of payment: offer emergency cash collection at a branch.

## 9. Resolution outcomes
- Card blocked, device removed, replacement ordered, and any later transactions referred to fraud.

## 10. Record keeping
Keep the report time, actions taken, approvals and delivery details for 7 years.
