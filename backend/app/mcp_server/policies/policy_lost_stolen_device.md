# Lost or stolen device or card

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

## Common causes
- Theft
- Loss in public transport or taxis
- Misplaced at home or work

## Standard checks
- Block the card and revoke app sessions on the lost device
- Check for transactions after the reported loss time
- Verify the customer's identity through a secondary channel

## Standard response
A human officer blocks the card and sessions, confirms what was protected, and arranges a replacement card or device registration.

## Reference values (sample)
- A replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days, free of charge when reported lost or stolen.
- Blocking a card or revoking app sessions takes effect immediately.
