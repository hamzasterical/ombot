# Unauthorized transaction

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

## Common causes
- Credential theft or phishing
- SIM swap
- Card details compromised
- Use by a family member

## Standard checks
- Confirm the transaction details, channel and device ID
- Compare the device and location with the customer's history
- Check for recent password, phone or beneficiary changes
- Freeze the account or payment if fraud is likely (human approval required)

## Standard response
A human officer contacts the customer, confirms the account is secured, and explains the investigation steps and timeline. Do not promise a refund before the investigation ends.

## Reference values (sample)
- The customer is told the outcome of a provisional credit decision within 10 business days.
- A full investigation can take up to 45 days.
