# Wrong recipient transfer

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

| Policy ID | Owner | Risk tier | Version | Review cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| policy_wrong_recipient | Payments Operations | Moderate | 2.3 | Every 12 months |

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers a customer who sent money to the wrong person or account by mistake, for example by mistyping an account number or choosing the wrong saved contact. It applies to transfers within OmBank, domestic transfers to other banks and international wires. If the customer was tricked into paying a fraudster, treat it as an authorized push payment scam under the unauthorized transaction policy instead.

**How customers describe it:** "I sent money to the wrong account"; "I typed the wrong account number"; "I picked the wrong contact".

## 2. Definitions
- **Recall request:** a formal request from OmBank to the receiving bank to return the funds.
- **Misdirected payment:** a payment that reached an account the customer did not intend, through the customer's own error.

## 3. Common causes
- Mistyped account number or IBAN
- Wrong contact selected from the saved beneficiaries list
- Outdated beneficiary details (the recipient has changed banks or accounts)
- Confirmation of payee warning ignored

## 4. Mandatory investigation steps
1. Confirm the transaction ID, amount, date and the recipient account.
2. Ask the customer for the intended recipient's correct details, to show the error.
3. Check whether the funds are still pending or already credited.
4. If still pending, try to stop the payment before it settles.
5. Check whether the recipient account is at OmBank (an internal recovery) or at another bank (an external recall).
6. Raise a recall request and record the reference.

## 5. Authority matrix
| Action | Who may decide |
|---|---|
| Read-only checks, draft reply | Automated agent |
| Stop a pending payment | Payments officer (human approval required) |
| Raise a recall request | Payments officer |
| Debit an internal recipient's account | Not permitted without the recipient's written consent |
| Goodwill credit for bank error, such as a missing confirmation-of-payee warning | Payments team lead, up to $250 |

## 6. Customer communication
- Explain that recovery depends on the transfer status and, once funds are credited, on the recipient's consent.
- Never promise that the money will be returned. Say what OmBank will do and when the customer will next hear from us.
- Do not share the recipient's name or contact details; this is protected under privacy rules.
- If the recipient refuses, explain the customer's option to pursue the matter through the courts, and offer the information OmBank can lawfully provide.

## 7. Timelines (sample)
- A recall request is raised within 1 business day of the report.
- Recipient banks usually answer a recall within 10 business days.
- Funds already credited to the recipient can only be returned with the recipient's consent.
- For an internal recipient, OmBank contacts them within 2 business days.

## 8. Escalation triggers
- The amount is above $5,000, or the customer is vulnerable: escalate to the payments team lead.
- Signs that the customer was deceived (a "new account details" email or an urgent request): reclassify as a scam.
- The recipient bank does not reply within 10 business days: send a reminder and escalate.

## 9. Resolution outcomes
- **Recovered:** funds returned to the customer's account, without fees.
- **Partly recovered or refused:** written explanation of the outcome and of the customer's options.

## 10. Record keeping
Keep recall references, correspondence and decisions for 7 years.
