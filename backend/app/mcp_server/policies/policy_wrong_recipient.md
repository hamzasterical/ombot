# Wrong recipient transfer

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

## Common causes
- Mistyped account number or IBAN
- Wrong contact selected
- Outdated beneficiary details

## Standard checks
- Confirm the transaction ID, amount and recipient account
- Check whether the funds are still pending or already credited
- Check whether the recipient is within the bank

## Standard response
Explain that recovery depends on the recipient's consent or the transfer status. Start a recall request where possible and share the expected timeline.

## Reference values (sample)
- A recall request is raised within 1 business day of the report.
- Recipient banks usually answer a recall within 10 business days.
- Funds already credited to the recipient can only be returned with the recipient's consent.
