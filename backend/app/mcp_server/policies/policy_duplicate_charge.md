# Duplicate charge or merchant dispute

> Sample bank policy. Synthetic content for demonstration only; not real regulation.

## Common causes
- Merchant double billing
- Retry after a timeout that was captured twice
- Cancelled subscription still billing
- Goods or services not delivered

## Standard checks
- Compare both charges: amount, time, merchant ID
- Check the merchant's complaint history
- Ask the customer for proof of cancellation, return or non-delivery

## Standard response
Open a dispute with the merchant. Refund the duplicate charge if confirmed, and explain the dispute timeline for other cases.

## Reference values (sample)
- A confirmed duplicate charge is refunded within 5 business days.
- Merchant disputes (non-delivery, cancelled subscription, refund not received) take up to 45 days.
